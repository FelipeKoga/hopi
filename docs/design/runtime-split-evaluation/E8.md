**Numbering.** E8 has no numbered sub-items, and one passage often covers several of its topics (ARCHITECTURE 74-239 covers eight of the eleven). So the answer is numbered per document, in the order E8 names them: 1 README, 2 ARCHITECTURE, 3 ROADMAP, 4-8 `docs/design/*.md`. The topic index below maps E8's topics, in the plan's order, to rows.

  - **touch up**: change one clause or name.
